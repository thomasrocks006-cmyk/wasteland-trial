enemy sculpts — where they came from
====================================

These are Lord of the Rings film-design sculpts (New Line / Weta's designs), found as
3D-print models and reduced for the web. They are fan-sourced assets: fine for this game,
not licensed for commercial release without checking with the rights holders.

what was done to them
  Loaded, print plinths and backdrop cards stripped, stood up and scaled to metres
  (orc 1.85, uruk ~2.0, trolls 3.6-4.6, ringwraith 2.1), decimated to 2.8k-20k tris,
  and vertex-coloured (skin ramp darkened into creases). No textures, no skeleton: they
  are static sculpts. The conversion script is in the round's scratch dir, not here.

licence reality check
  PJ's orcs, Uruks and trolls are Weta designs. Every model here is either a fan sculpt of
  that design or a print-model derived from it, so the shape is close to the films and the
  licence is not clean. If this ever ships commercially, treat these as placeholders with
  a paper trail, not as cleared assets.
